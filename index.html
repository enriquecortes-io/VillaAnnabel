Subject History · Declassified

SHE WORE
BALENCIAGA.
And carried a pistol.

Built in the 1950s for Carmen Franco, daughter of the Spanish Generalísimo. Acquired a decade later by Aline Griffith — American OSS field agent, New York model, and Countess of Romanones — sent to Madrid under cover to infiltrate Nazi networks within Spain's neutral wartime élite.

Griffith moved through the highest circles of European society. Dressed in Balenciaga. Rubies at her throat. A pistol in her bag. Villa Annabel became her stage — hosting the most glamorous gatherings Marbella has ever seen.

In 1989, the U.S. Army inducted her into its Military Intelligence Hall of Fame. Her bestselling memoir: The Spy Wore Red, 1987.
