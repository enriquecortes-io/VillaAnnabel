The difference in dates between the historical record and the cadastral one is quite common and can be due to several reasons.

Sometimes, the Cadastre records the year a major renovation or an extension was completed, rather than the original construction date.

It could also be that the year in the registry corresponds to the date the building was legalized or when a cadastral update was formalized.

Sometimes, they simply assigned an approximate year due to a lack of exact data during the formation of the Cadastre.
